      from assets/cropsnh/textures/blocks/crops/melon/4.png

  The fruit in those two images reuses pixels of Minecraft's pumpkin_side and
  melon_side textures, which are not CropsNH's to license. The *_vine images are
  the originals with those pixels removed. The *_fruit images mark the removed
  pixels and store, in their red and green channels, which vanilla pixel each one
  showed. The game redraws stage 4 from these and the player's own vanilla
  textures, so no Minecraft pixels ship here.
